Some people are simply impossible to walk away from. Even when every story warns against it, the heart still turns around, hoping for one more glimpse.

Some loves are not undone by the absence of faith, but by the quiet certainty that one last look is worth whatever comes after.

Kindness became something I did without thinking. Holding the door, a little longer. Waiting until someone got home safely. Remembering the little things people forgot, I remembered. It became second nature, as though my hands had learned to reach out before my mind had the chance to ask why.

I rarely kept count of what it cost.
Sometimes it meant arriving home later than I planned. Sometimes it meant carrying feelings that had nowhere to go. Sometimes it simply meant walking away with less than I had quietly given.

Yet I kept doing it.
Not because I expected anything in return.
There was just something about those small moments that made the world feel a little less distant.
